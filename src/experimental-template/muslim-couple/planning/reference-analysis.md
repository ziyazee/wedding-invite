## Scene Structure

This is a **single continuous shot** with no scene cuts, no typography, and no overlaid text. The entire video is one slow forward camera dolly through a Mughal-style garden terrace at sunset with a Muslim wedding couple.

### Phase 1: Establishing Shot (0:00 - 0:02)

**Frames 1-2 (0s, 1s)**

The video opens with a wide establishing shot of an ornate white marble terrace. No people are visible yet. The composition is:

- **Left side**: A grand Mughal pointed arch (ogee arch) with white marble columns, draped in cascading pink and white flowers (roses and bougainvillea). The arch pillar features carved Islamic geometric relief patterns.
- **Top edge**: Floral garlands of pink blossoms and green foliage drape across the entire top of the frame, creating a natural floral arch that frames the sky.
- **Center / Background**: A landscape vista opens up — a marble walkway leads past ornamental lanterns toward a reflecting pool and a multi-tiered water fountain. Beyond the water, a distant **mosque with a large central dome and two tall minarets** sits against the sunset-lit horizon. Mountains are visible in the far distance. Palm trees and cypress trees dot the garden landscape.
- **Right side**: A stone balustrade with carved Islamic geometric cutout patterns lines the terrace edge, with more pink roses and lanterns.
- **Floor**: White polished marble with scattered rose petals in pink and deep rose.
- **Sky**: A magnificent golden-hour sunset gradient — soft lavender-blue at the top, transitioning through salmon-pink and peach to deep gold at the horizon line.

The mood is grand, inviting, and dreamlike — the scene looks like a Mughal paradise garden.

A small **four-pointed star sparkle** effect appears near the lower-right area of the frame.

### Phase 2: Couple Enters (0:02 - 0:04)

**Frames 3-4 (2s, 3s)**

As the camera slowly dollies forward, a couple rises into frame from the bottom. They are seen entirely **from behind**, walking away from the viewer toward the sunset vista.

- **Groom** (left): Wearing a cream/ivory **sherwani** (long coat) over white churidar trousers. The sherwani has subtle embroidery work. Dark shoes. Dark hair. His posture is upright and composed.
- **Bride** (right): Wearing a blush-champagne **lehenga** with a long trailing skirt. A matching **dupatta/hijab** covers her head and drapes over her shoulders and back. The outfit features delicate floral embroidery and scattered small embellishments throughout the fabric. The dupatta fabric is sheer/semi-transparent with embroidered floral motifs.

The couple walks side by side but not yet holding hands. Their scale in frame is roughly half-height, occupying the lower portion.

The four-pointed sparkle effect continues near lower-right.

### Phase 3: Walking Together (0:04 - 0:06)

**Frames 5-6 (4s, 5s)**

The couple now occupies a more prominent position in frame. They are:

- Walking forward together, now **holding hands**
- The trailing fabric of the bride's lehenga sweeps across the marble floor
- More detail visible on the groom's sherwani — structured collar, fitted silhouette
- The bride's embroidered dupatta flows elegantly behind her

The camera has moved far enough forward that:
- The top floral framing has thinned slightly (we've passed under more of the arch)
- The arch column on the left is more cropped
- The mosque and minarets in the background are more central and prominent
- Flower petals on the floor catch warm reflections from the sunset light

The overall composition centers the couple as the focal point, framed by architecture on the left and flowers on both sides.

### Phase 4: Emotional Peak (0:06 - 0:08)

**Frames 7-8 (6s, 7s)**

This is the most emotionally resonant segment:

- The couple has moved further forward and is now a medium shot
- The groom **turns his head toward the bride**, looking at her
- The bride **raises her right hand, pointing toward the mosque and sunset**
- Their left hands remain linked
- This gesture creates a beautiful narrative moment — the bride showing something meaningful on the horizon

Background changes:
- The sunset has deepened, more golden-amber tones
- The mosque appears more illuminated, as if lit from within
- The overall scene is warmer

### Phase 5: Final Gaze (0:08 - 0:10)

**Frames 9-11 (8s, 9s, 10s)**

The closing moments:

- The couple stands together, still linked by hands
- The bride's hand remains pointed toward the horizon
- In later frames, **small birds** become visible in the sky, adding to the romantic atmosphere
- The camera continues its subtle forward push
- The floral framing at top is now sparse — just hanging blossoms at the edges
- The arch column on the left occupies less frame space
- More of the sunset vista is visible
- Rose petals on the ground are more prominent in the warmer light

The final frame shows the couple from behind in medium shot, gazing together at the sunset, mosque, and the infinite horizon. A bird is visible in the sky. The mood is one of peace, unity, and a new beginning.

- Foreground framing (arch, top florals) moving fastest
- The couple serving as the primary focal anchor

The animation philosophy is one of **restraint and subtlety** — almost everything is still or moving very slowly. Motion is used to suggest life, not to demand attention.

### Template Animation Recommendations
- Use very slow, gentle animations (1000-2000ms durations)
- Ease-out or ease-in-out timing functions (no bouncing, no snapping)
- Parallax at subtle rates (0.02-0.05 multiplier)
- Text reveals: gentle fade-up, 800-1200ms
- Sparkle particles: small, rare, soft (not a blizzard)
- No aggressive scroll-jacking or rapid transitions

## Composition Analysis

The composition follows a classic **one-point perspective** with the vanishing point at the mosque/sunset:

```
+---[floral garland across top]---+
|   flowers    |  SKY   | flowers |
|  ARCH        |        |         |
|  COLUMN      | COUPLE | RAILING |
|  + flowers   |        | +flower |
|  + lanterns  | MARBLE | +lanter |
|              | FLOOR  |         |
+---------------------------------+
```

Key compositional principles:
- **Framing**: The arch and florals create a natural frame within the frame
- **Leading lines**: The marble walkway, balustrade, and lantern lines all converge toward the mosque/sunset
- **Rule of thirds**: The couple is positioned at the lower-third intersection
- **Depth**: Five distinct depth layers create a strong sense of three-dimensionality
- **Symmetry**: Near-symmetrical left-right composition (arch on left, railing on right)
- **Focal hierarchy**: Couple > Mosque > Sunset > Florals > Architecture

## Key Takeaways for Template Design

1. **Single-scene narrative, not multi-scene montage** — the invitation should feel like one continuous experience, not a slideshow
2. **Parallax scrolling is natural** — the depth layers are perfectly set up for scroll-driven parallax
3. **Warm blush-gold-ivory palette** — stay within this range; avoid cool blues or bold saturated colors
4. **Mughal architectural framing** — arches and florals as section frames and dividers
5. **Lanterns as recurring decorative motif** — warm, glowing, evenly spaced
6. **Scattered rose petals** — a ground texture element that can repeat in sections
7. **Mosque silhouette** — the primary cultural anchor, should appear at least once
8. **Subtle sparkle effects** — small, rare, four-pointed stars, not overwhelming
9. **No text in the video** — typography design is independent, should complement the mood
10. **Slow, gentle animation timing** — everything breathes, nothing snaps
11. **The couple is shown from behind** — preserving anonymity, making it universalizable for any couple
12. **Sunset as emotional backdrop** — warmth, golden hour, the feeling of a new beginning
