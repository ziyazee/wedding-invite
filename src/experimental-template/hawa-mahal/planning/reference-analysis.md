## Architecture and Decorative Elements

### Building Structure
- **Five tiers** of decreasing width, creating a pyramidal/stepped profile
- The building is wider at the base and narrows toward the top
- Each tier features a row of **bay windows (jharokhas)** — semi-octagonal protruding window bays
- The jharokhas are the signature element: each is a small projecting enclosed balcony with arched openings
- Between and around the jharokhas are recessed wall panels with decorative carvings

### Architectural Details (per tier, bottom to top)
1. **Ground floor (Tier 1):** Largest arches, thick cylindrical pillars (semi-columns), grand central entrance arch. The most heavily illuminated tier.
2. **Second floor (Tier 2):** Slightly smaller jharokhas. Honeycomb/hexagonal jali lattice screens visible in windows. Decorated cornices with carved motifs.
3. **Third floor (Tier 3):** Similar to Tier 2 but narrower. Arched window openings with lattice screens. Small domed canopies above some windows.
4. **Fourth floor (Tier 4):** Narrower tier with smaller jharokhas. More closely spaced windows.
5. **Crown/Top floor (Tier 5):** Smallest and narrowest. Crowned with multiple small domed finials and pointed spires. The most ornate silhouette against the sky.

### Window Types
1. **Arched windows with jali lattice:** Honeycomb/hexagonal pattern screens that glow teal-green when backlit
2. **Rectangular windows with grid screens:** White lattice in a rectangular grid pattern, glowing warm gold when lit
3. **Open arched windows:** Without lattice, showing the dark interior or warm glow

### Decorative Motifs
- **Cusped/pointed arches (mehrab arches):** Multi-lobed arch forms above windows
- **Scalloped cornices:** Decorative ledge edges between tiers
- **Carved relief panels:** Floral and geometric patterns in stone between windows
- **Semi-cylindrical columns:** Pilaster-like columns flanking each jharokha bay
- **Small domed canopies (chhatris):** Crown the roofline and some window bays
- **Finials and spires:** Small pointed pinnacles at the very top of the roofline
- **Lotus/floral base motifs:** Carved at the base of columns and below windows

## Key Takeaways for Template Design

8. **Static camera, frontal composition** — the visual interest comes entirely from lighting animation and pyrotechnic effects, not camera movement
