# muslim-royal — Cultural direction

Template `muslim-royal`, route `/experimental/muslim-royal`, prefix `mr`, component `MuslimRoyal`.
Tradition: South Asian Muslim nikah and walima, shown as a royal Mughal-court celebration. Feeling: white marble, emerald and gold leaf; blue-hour lanterns; hush and grandeur, not noise.
Language: English with romanized Urdu/Arabic phrases. Real Arabic script is set as live text (Amiri / Reem Kufi), never baked into raster art.

## Faith guardrails
- Islamic art is aniconic in sacred contexts: no people, no animals, no idols anywhere in the art.
- Bismillah and the nikah dua are Quranic/prophetic text. They sit at the head of the page and in the closing, on clean ground, never on anything walked on, cut or decorative-only (never on a form control, never repeated as wallpaper).
- Do not depict the Kaaba, Quran pages, mihrab prayer niches or a mosque interior. Domes, minarets, arches, jali and pavilions are architecture and are fine.
- Crescent (hilal) only as a finial on a dome or plaque and as a small sprite. No star-and-crescent flag.
- No music in the sample (many families prefer none). The music button is omitted.
- Dress code for the invitation copy: "Nikah" is the contract; "Walima" the groom's-side feast; "Mehndi", "Dholki", "Mangni" are cultural. Never call anything a "ceremony of light" or similar.

## Architecture and ornament vocabulary (subjects come from here, not from the opening)
- Multifoil (cusped) arch, iwan portal, jharokha balcony, chhatri pavilion, onion dome with lotus-base and finial, slender minaret, jali (pierced marble screen), charbagh quadrant garden with a long reflecting channel, cypress and fountains.
- Pietra dura marble inlay: tulip, iris, poppy, lily stems in lapis, carnelian, jade, mother-of-pearl, on white marble.
- Zardozi (gold-thread embroidery) on emerald and burgundy velvet; qanat and shamiana tent panels; brocade kinkhab; bolsters (gao takiya) on a takht.
- Geometry: khatam (eight-pointed star), girih strapwork, islimi arabesque, muqarnas honeycomb steps.
- Objects: brass fanous lanterns, silver paandaan, attar bottles, gulab-pash rosewater sprinkler, ittar and dates (khajoor) on a silver tray, dholak, mehndi cone, nikahnama scroll with qalam, ring box, handi of biryani, sheer khurma.
- Flowers: mogra (jasmine) and gulab (rose) strands: the *phoolon ki chaadar* / sehra curtain. Marigold is avoided (belongs to the Punjabi template).
- Botany: cypress, tulips, irises, roses.

## Palette (target)
- Ivory marble `#F6EEDC`, bone `#EADFC6`, shadow marble `#D9CBAA`.
- Emerald `#0F5B47` (ink for headings), deep emerald `#0A3A31`, night `#0A1B2E`, sapphire dusk `#1D3B66`.
- Gold leaf `#C9A24B`, bright gold `#E8C873`, antique gold text-safe `#8A6A22`.
- Ruby `#7B1230` (rare accent), pearl `#FBF7EE`, walnut ink `#2B2018`.
- Scenes are **blue hour** (night sapphire to teal, warm lantern gold); page sections are **marble day-ivory**. Text on scenes is ivory/gold; text on marble is emerald/walnut.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (500/600 and italic) for names and headings.
- Caps/labels: Cinzel.
- Body: Cormorant Garamond 500 (18px+ for legibility).
- Arabic: Amiri (naskh) for Bismillah and dua; Reem Kufi for geometric headings.

## Sections (same architecture as punjabi-heritage, re-composed)
1. **Opening** — marble iwan facing a charbagh at blue hour; mogra-and-rose curtain across the top; Bismillah in calligraphy, then names. Layered parallax: far plate (sky, domes, garden, cypress) vs near plate (iwan, curtain, lanterns, steps).
2. **Couple — "Jodi"** — zardozi qanat valance; family cards with an arch-crest on the top edge and pietra-dura corners; connector is a khatam star carrying ما شاء الله.
3. **Countdown — "Intezaar"** — night courtyard scene, four multifoil-niche plaques with crescent finials, a girih rail that draws on, إن شاء الله line.
4. **Celebrations — "Jashn"** — sehra toran, flexible cards, khatam date plaque, five motifs (mangni, mehndi, dholki, nikah, walima) matched by title keywords.
5. **RSVP — "Tashreef Laiye"** — royal majlis tableau (takht, bolsters, paandaan, attar, dates, lantern) over a calm marble reply card; Arabic welcome.
6. **Closing — "Duas"** — charbagh at night through a marble arch; dua in Amiri over the clear sky; names and date.

## Sample content (Hyderabad / Lucknow families)
Ayaan Qureshi & Zoya Siddiqui. Parents: Mr. Imran & Mrs. Shaheen Qureshi (Banjara Hills, Hyderabad); Mr. Tariq & Mrs. Farzana Siddiqui (Hazratganj, Lucknow). Wedding 17 April 2027 (outside Ramadan). Events: Mangni, Mehndi & Dholki, Nikah, Walima.
