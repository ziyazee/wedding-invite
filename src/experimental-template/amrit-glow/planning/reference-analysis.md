# Amrit Glow - Reference Analysis

**Template**: amrit-glow
**Cultural context**: Punjabi cultural (less religious) - Golden Temple night aesthetic
**Source**: 10s video (720x1280, 24fps H.264), 3 extracted key frames
**Date**: 2026-11-13

---

## 1. Overall Design Identity

The reference video establishes a **cinematic nighttime grandeur** aesthetic built around the Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) viewed across the Amrit Sarovar. The progression from moonlit darkness to warm golden illumination to celebratory fireworks creates a natural three-act narrative that maps perfectly onto a wedding invitation's emotional arc: anticipation, revelation, celebration. The aesthetic is distinctly Punjabi in cultural origin but reads as universal luxury - golden architecture reflected in dark water under a crescent moon. The tone is warm, opulent, and reverent without being overtly religious; it celebrates the grandeur of Punjabi heritage through architectural beauty and light rather than iconography. This positions the template as aspirational and cinematic, suitable for couples who want cultural resonance with contemporary elegance.

## 2. Composition & Layout

### Frame 1 (0.5s - Dark/Moonlit)
- **Layout**: Vertical rule-of-thirds with temple centered in lower 60%, moon in upper-right third
- **Negative space**: ~40% of frame is dark sky, creating dramatic breathing room
- **Focal hierarchy**: Moon (brightest) -> Temple silhouette -> Water (darkest)
- **Temple position**: Centered horizontally, vertically positioned at roughly 35-65% of frame height
- **Water band**: Bottom ~20% of frame, nearly black with minimal reflections

### Frame 2 (3s - Temple Lit)
- **Shift**: Temple becomes primary focal point as illumination reveals detail
- **Golden mass**: Temple occupies ~35% of total frame area
- **Reflection**: Water now shows ~15% of frame with golden reflections creating a mirror axis
- **Moon**: Maintains position, now secondary to temple glow
- **Light points**: Small lanterns along the perimeter walkway create a constellation of warm dots
- **Symmetry**: Near-perfect bilateral symmetry of temple + reflection

### Frame 3 (8s - Fully Lit + Fireworks)
- **Maximum density**: Fireworks fill upper 35% of frame, temple in center, reflections below
- **Three horizontal bands**: Fireworks/sky (top 35%), Temple/architecture (middle 40%), Water/reflections (bottom 25%)
- **Energy direction**: Radial burst from fireworks above + vertical column of reflected light below
- **Balance**: Fireworks break the symmetry with organic burst patterns while temple maintains centered stability

### Key Layout Principles for Template
- **Vertical tripartite division**: Sky/decorative top, content middle, reflective/ambient bottom
- **Center-weighted symmetry**: Content anchored to center with ornamental elements at margins
- **Progressive reveal**: Empty-to-full density mirrors scroll progression
- **Generous negative space in dark palette**: Dark backgrounds can carry 40%+ empty space without feeling bare

## 3. Architecture & Decorative Elements

### Architectural Features Observed
| Element | Description | Cultural Context |
|---------|-------------|------------------|
| **Dome** | Central gilded dome with smaller subsidiary domes | Sikh gurdwara architecture, derived from Mughal-Rajput synthesis |
| **Chhatris** | Small domed kiosks at corners and parapet | Rajput architectural tradition, marks of royalty |
| **Jharokha windows** | Arched window bays with decorative frames | Mughal-Rajput balcony windows |
| **Parapet crenellations** | Decorative roofline with repeated small pointed elements | Indo-Islamic architectural ornament |
| **Perimeter walkway** | Low balustrade with evenly-spaced pillars and oil lamps | Parikrama - the circumambulation path |
| **Water body** | Still reflecting pool surrounding the structure | Amrit Sarovar (Pool of Nectar) |
| **Crescent moon** | Prominent in all 3 frames, upper-right quadrant | Cultural/celestial - night, auspiciousness |

### Decorative Motifs to Extract
- **Scalloped/multi-foil arch profiles**: The cusped arches visible on windows and doors - these map to the `arch-border.png` asset
- **Floral scrollwork**: Gilded floral patterns on panels and friezes
- **Geometric jali patterns**: Latticed panels visible in mid-frame
- **Finials**: Pointed ornamental tops on domes and towers
- **Reflecting symmetry**: Architecture mirrored in water is itself a visual motif

### Cultural Positioning (Less Religious)
The reference leans on architectural grandeur and celebratory spectacle (fireworks, light show) rather than religious symbolism. The Golden Temple is presented as a landmark of Punjabi pride and aesthetic beauty. For the template, abstract from specific identifiable religious architecture into: gold ornamental arches, water reflections, celestial elements (moon/stars), festive pyrotechnics, and oil lamps.

## 5. Typography Suggestions

### Based on Visual Language
The reference's visual vocabulary suggests typography that is:
- **Regal but not medieval**: Gold architectural ornament demands serif display type with modern proportions
- **High contrast**: Dark background + luminous text means typefaces should have good weight range
- **Legible at low contrast**: Cream-on-dark-navy needs generous x-height for body text

### Brief Selections Validated

| Font | Role | Assessment |
|------|------|------------|
| **Cinzel Decorative** | Display (couple names, headers) | Strong match. Roman capitals with decorative serifs echo the architectural ornament. The all-caps nature matches the temple's symmetrical grandeur. Caution: may feel heavy at large sizes - use letter-spacing of 0.1-0.15em. |
| **Cormorant Garamond** | Headings (section titles, event names) | Excellent match. High-contrast old-style serif with elegant thin strokes that mirror the delicate gold filigree. Light/Regular weights recommended to keep the airy feel. |
| **Lato** | Body (event details, addresses) | Practical choice. Clean sans-serif ensures readability for small text on dark backgrounds. Light or Regular weight, 16-18px minimum at mobile viewport. |

### Additional Typographic Recommendations
- **Display tracking**: Cinzel Decorative at hero size should use `letter-spacing: 0.12em` to avoid letter crowding
- **Line heights**: 1.2 for Cinzel display, 1.4 for Cormorant headings, 1.6 for Lato body
- **Fallback stack**: `"Cinzel Decorative", "Playfair Display", Georgia, serif` / `"Cormorant Garamond", "EB Garamond", Georgia, serif` / `Lato, "Source Sans 3", system-ui, sans-serif`
- **No Gurmukhi script needed**: Cultural context is Punjabi but presentation language is English - no bilingual font pairing required unless user requests Punjabi text

## 7. Lighting & Atmosphere

### Light Sources (by frame)

**Frame 1**: Single light source - moon. Provides rim light on temple dome and upper surfaces. Ambient fill is near-zero. Mood: mysterious, anticipatory, intimate.

**Frame 2**: Multiple sources - moon (now secondary) + temple illumination (primary) + perimeter oil lamps (accent). The temple glows from within and from spotlights, creating a warm golden mass against the cold dark sky. The water catches golden reflections in broken horizontal bands. Mood: revealed beauty, warmth against darkness, awe.

**Frame 3**: Maximum sources - moon + temple + lamps + fireworks (overhead fill). Fireworks add a bright overhead wash that partially illuminates the sky and creates additional sparkle reflections on water. The entire scene is bathed in warm gold. Mood: celebration, maximum energy, joyful climax.

### Light Characteristics
| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Color temperature | ~2700-3000K (very warm, candlelight to warm tungsten) |
| Dominant light direction | Internal/omnidirectional from temple, overhead from moon/fireworks |
| Shadow quality | Soft/diffused - no harsh shadows visible |
| Specular highlights | Water surface catches discrete bright points; firework sparks are point-sources |
| Ambient ratio | Frame 1: 1:8 (high contrast), Frame 2: 1:4 (moderate), Frame 3: 1:3 (filled) |
| Bloom/glow | Significant around moon, temple peaks, firework cores - suggests using CSS/SVG glow effects |

### Atmosphere Implementation
- **Background gradient**: Not flat `#0F1623` but a subtle radial gradient - slightly lighter at center (where temple would be) fading to darker edges
- **Glow effects**: `box-shadow` or radial gradients with gold-to-transparent for element lighting
- **Depth haze**: Distant buildings in Frame 3 show atmospheric perspective - lighter, less saturated. Use this for layered parallax elements
- **Water shimmer**: The broken reflection pattern suggests an animated CSS shimmer or parallax-shifted reflection layer

## 8. Materials & Textures

### Observed Materials
| Material | Where Seen | Hex Range | Texture Quality |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Gilded metal** | Temple surfaces, dome | `#D4A843` to `#8B6914` | Smooth with subtle grain; specular highlights on edges |
| **Marble/stone** | Walkway balustrade, base | `#C8BFA8` to `#E0D5C0` | Matte, slightly warm |
| **Dark water** | Sarovar surface | `#0A0E16` to `#1A2030` | Reflective but broken/rippled; not mirror-smooth |
| **Night sky** | Upper frame | `#0C1320` to `#151E2E` | Smooth gradient, very slight texture/noise |
| **Warm light glow** | Moon, lamps, fireworks | `#FFE4A0` to `#FFF8E8` | Soft-edged, bloom-like, no hard boundaries |

### Texture Implementation for Template
- **Gold surfaces**: Use subtle `linear-gradient` with 2-3 gold stops for metallic feel; avoid flat fills
- **Dark backgrounds**: Add very faint noise texture (1-2% opacity) to avoid banding on gradients
- **Water texture**: The water-reflection.png asset provides a good base; overlay at 30-50% opacity as a section background
- **Architectural borders**: The arch-border.png provides ornamental framing; event-card-bg.png provides content card surfaces
- **Glow/bloom**: Use `filter: blur()` on pseudo-elements or SVG feGaussianBlur for light bloom effects around diyas, sparkles, and the moon

### Material Contrast Strategy
The template's visual richness comes from juxtaposing:
1. **Matte dark** (sky/background) vs **luminous gold** (architectural elements)
2. **Sharp geometric** (architecture, arches) vs **organic soft** (water, glow, flowers)
3. **Static/solid** (cards, text) vs **ambient/moving** (sparkles, leaves, reflections)

This three-layer contrast system (tone, geometry, motion) should guide all design decisions.
