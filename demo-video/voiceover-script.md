# Voiceover Script — Shaadi Invites Demo Video (v2)

Voice: Alice turbo (ElevenLabs, clear, engaging, British)
Target: ~82 seconds, no overlapping segments, one continuous narrator
Tone: Warm, convincing, selling ease-of-use

---

## Scene 1: Hook (0–6s)
**[0.5s]** "What if your store could create stunning digital wedding invitations — in under five minutes?"

## Scene 2: Dedicated Dashboard (6–14s)
**[6.5s]** "When a store joins Shaadi Invites, they get their own dashboard — with live order tracking, revenue insights, and a complete template collection."

## Scene 3: Store Landing Page + Templates (14–23s)
**[14.5s]** "Every store also gets a branded landing page — like elegantcards.shaadiinvites.in — that you share with customers, so they can browse and choose a design themselves."

## Scene 4: Pricing Freedom (23–27s)
**[23.5s]** "And you set your own prices. Your margins, your rules."

## Scene 5: AI Upload — Star Feature (27–40s)
**[27.5s]** "Now, here's where it gets exciting. Upload the soft copy of any physical wedding card — and our AI reads every detail. Names, dates, venues — filled into the form automatically, in seconds."

## Scene 6: Live Preview + Edit (40–50s)
**[40.5s]** "The invitation comes alive on a real phone preview, right beside the form. Drag to reposition text, resize it, tweak anything visually. Even Google Maps links are detected automatically from the venue."

## Scene 7: Publish + WhatsApp + Customer Self-Service (50–62s)
**[50.5s]** "One click to publish. The invitation link goes straight to the customer on WhatsApp. And they also get a management link — so they can update names, dates, or events on their own, anytime. No more back and forth."

## Scene 8: Extras — Music, Language, Multi-Invitation (62–74s)
**[62.5s]** "There's more. Choose from a library of songs or upload a custom track. Switch languages instantly. And create multiple invitations from a single order — Wedding, Mehendi, Reception — each as its own shareable link."

## Scene 9: CTA — Onboard (74–82s)
**[74.5s]** "Onboard your store to Shaadi Invites today. Eighteen premium templates, AI-powered creation, and everything your customers need — all in one platform. Visit shaadiinvites.in to get started."
